Organizing is not the same job as cleaning and it should not be priced like it. This is billed hourly because the honest answer to how long a pantry takes is that it depends entirely on what is in the pantry.

The goal is a system you can actually keep. Beautiful bins that need forty minutes a week to maintain are a failure. If it does not survive a bad Tuesday, it was not the right system.

What is included

  • Sort, group and declutter
  • Donation pile bagged and hauled
  • Containers sized to the space
  • Clear labeling that lasts
  • Pantry and food rotation
  • Closet reset by category
  • Garage and storage zones
  • Kids rooms and toy systems
  • Paperwork sorted and filed
  • A simple plan to keep it that way

How it works

Three steps, in order.

Everything comes out

You cannot organize around things you have not looked at. The space gets emptied completely before a single decision is made.

You make the keep calls

Nothing of yours gets thrown away by someone else. I sort into categories and hand you the fast decisions; you keep control of the hard ones.

Zones, then labels

Things get grouped by how you actually use them rather than how they look on a shelf, and then labeled so the system survives other people putting things away.
