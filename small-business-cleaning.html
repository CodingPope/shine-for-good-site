Small commercial spaces get quoted badly by big companies, either priced like a house or priced like a hospital. This is priced for what it is: a real space with real hours and a standing schedule.

One consistent person, working after you close, on a checklist you approve. No rotating crews, no different standard every week, no strangers with your alarm code.

What is included

  • Restrooms fully sanitized
  • Breakroom and kitchenette
  • Entry glass and door handles
  • Desks and shared surfaces
  • Floors vacuumed and mopped
  • Trash and recycling out
  • Supply restocking
  • Waiting areas reset
  • Flexible nights and weekends
  • Standing weekly or biweekly

How it works

Three steps, in order.

Walk the space together

Fifteen minutes on site beats any online form. I need to see your floors, your restrooms, and how your team actually uses the place.

Agree the checklist

You get a written scope of exactly what happens each visit and what happens monthly. Nothing is assumed on either side.

Standing schedule, invoiced monthly

Same nights every week, one invoice at month end, net 15. Simple enough to forget about, which is the point.
