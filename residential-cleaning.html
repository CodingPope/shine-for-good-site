This is the visit that keeps a house from ever getting bad. Kitchen, bathrooms, floors, dusting, beds, trash, all of it, on a standing day you never have to think about again.

The same person cleans your home every time. That matters more than people expect. By the third visit I know which cabinet sticks, which dog hides under the bed, and which corner the last cleaner kept skipping.

How it works

Three steps, in order.

Walk the house once

Before anything gets touched I go room to room and note what actually needs attention this visit. A house that hosted twelve people last weekend is not the same house it was two weeks ago.

Top down, wet last

Dust and cobwebs first, surfaces next, floors last. Cleaning in the wrong order means doing the floors twice, and nobody is paying for that.

Reset, not just clean

Cushions straightened, chairs pushed in, towels folded the way you fold them. The room should look like someone cared, not just like it got wiped.
