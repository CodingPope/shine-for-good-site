Deposits are rarely lost over big things. They are lost over the oven, the fridge seal, and the tops of the door frames, because those are the places a walkthrough always checks and a normal clean always skips.

This clean is built backwards from the inspection. If a property manager in Pinellas or Hillsborough is going to open it, pull it out, or run a finger along it, it is on the list.

What is included

  • Inside every cabinet and drawer
  • Inside the refrigerator
  • Inside the oven
  • Closets, shelves and rods
  • Appliance exteriors and sides
  • Wall spot cleaning
  • Baseboards and trim
  • Windows inside, sills and tracks
  • Light fixtures and fans
  • Garage sweep out
  • Floors detailed edge to edge
  • Final walkthrough with you

How it works

Three steps, in order.

Book it for after the truck

A move-out clean on a half packed apartment has to be redone and you pay twice. The right day is after the last box leaves and at least two days before the walkthrough.

Inspection order, not room order

Appliances and cabinet interiors first, because those are what get photographed. Floors last, once nothing else will drop dust on them.

Walk it together at the end

You see every room before I leave. If something needs another pass it happens then, not after you have handed back the keys.
